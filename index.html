BIOLOGY / CURRENT INTEREST

Chromosomal conditions

Trisomy 13 (Patau syndrome), Trisomy 18 (Edwards syndrome), and Trisomy 21 (Down syndrome) are associated with an additional copy of a chromosome. NIRO aims to explore credible scientific literature and educational context around these conditions. This page does not offer medical advice or imply that NIRO has developed a treatment or diagnostic tool.

TECHNOLOGY / CURRENT INTEREST

AGI & automated AI

AGI refers broadly to a still-unrealized or debated goal of systems with general capabilities across many tasks. Automated AI can include software agents and workflows that perform defined tasks with varying levels of oversight. NIRO's interest is exploratory; no claim is made here that NIRO has built AGI or deployed a validated AI system.
